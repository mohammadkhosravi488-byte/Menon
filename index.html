The course aim

Think like a chemist.

The study of Chemistry in Stage 6 enables students to develop an appreciation and understanding of materials and their properties, structures, interactions and related applications. Through applying Working Scientifically skills processes, the course aims to examine how chemical theories, models and practices are used and developed.

About the classroom

Chemistry is a way of seeing.

Meet the thinking behind Chemistry With Menon and the Stage 6 learning goals that shape this space.

Ms Laiju Menon

Teaching the connections, not just the content.

Chemistry With Menon gives senior students a clear place to revisit the ideas, methods and resources from their chemistry learning.

The teaching approach centres on making abstract chemical models understandable, linking evidence to explanation, and helping students communicate scientific reasoning with confidence.

From particles too small to see to systems that shape everyday life, chemistry rewards curiosity, precision and imagination.

Course objectives

What students develop.

The Stage 6 course brings together scientific skills, disciplinary knowledge and an informed appreciation of chemistry's place in society.

01 · Skills

Working Scientifically

  • Develop skills in applying the processes of Working Scientifically.
02 · Values

Relevance and responsibility

  • Develop positive, informed values and attitudes towards chemistry.
  • Recognise the importance and relevance of chemistry in their lives.
03 · Perspective

Science in society

  • Recognise the influence of economic, political and societal impacts on scientific knowledge.
  • Appreciate imagination and creativity in scientific research.

Year 11 knowledge

  • Develop knowledge and understanding of the fundamentals of chemistry.
  • Develop knowledge and understanding of the trends and driving forces in chemical interactions.

Year 12 knowledge

  • Develop knowledge and understanding of equilibrium and acid reactions.
  • Develop knowledge and understanding of the applications of chemistry.
